Fusion Sword Murasame Blade

Spell · Equip

Banlist: Unlimited (3 copies)

Edison text

No Edison-Accurate PSCT available for this card.

Printed text (newer wording, may work differently)
Equip only to a Warrior monster. It gains 800 ATK. While equipped to a monster, this card can't be destroyed by card effects.

Edison rulings

●If this card is face-down on the field, it can be destroyed by effects that destroy Spell Cards. ●When you activate this card, the opponent can chain a card like Magic Jammer that will negate its activation and destroy it. ●If you equip this card to Freed the Matchless General, Ryu Senshi or to a Warrior-Type monster you control when you have Frontier Wiseman on the field, it is negated and destroyed. ●If you equip this card to Gearfried the Iron Knight it is not destroyed. ●This card is not destroyed by cards that target, like Mystical Space Typhoon or Dust Tornado, or by cards that don't target, like Heavy Storm, Harpie’s Feather Duster, or Gryphon Wing. ●If a monster equipped with this card is flipped face-down, or removed from the field, this card is destroyed. ●This card can be sent from your hand or your Deck to the GY by the effect of Confiscation, Delinquent Duo, etc. ●If Imperial Order is active, this card can be destroyed by Dust Tornado, etc. ●This card can be returned to the owner’s hand by the effect of Giant Trunade or ‘Tornado Bird. ●If the monster this card is equipped to has its Type changed to anything but Warrior-Type, this card is destroyed. ●If the opponent chains Mystical Space Typhoon to the activation of this card, this card is destroyed before its effect can be applied.

General rulings
1. If this card is face-down on the field, it can be destroyed by effects that destroy Spell Cards. 2. When you activate this card, the opponent can chain a card like "Magic Jammer" that will negate its activation and destroy it. 3. If you equip this card to "Freed the Matchless General", "Ryu Senshi" or to a Warrior-Type monster you control when you have "Frontier Wiseman" on the field, it is negated and destroyed. 4. If you equip this card to "Gearfried the Iron Knight" it is not destroyed. 5. This card is not destroyed by cards that target, like "Mystical Space Typhoon" or "Dust Tornado", or by cards that do not target, like "Heavy Storm", "Harpie's Feather Duster", or "Gryphon Wing". 6. If a monster equipped with this card is flipped face-down, or removed from the field, this card is destroyed. 7. This card can be sent from your hand or your Deck to the GY by the effect of "Confiscation", "Delinquent Duo", etc. 8. If "Imperial Order" is active, this card can be destroyed by "Dust Tornado", etc. 9. This card can be returned to the owner's hand by the effect of "Giant Trunade" or "Tornado Bird". 10. If the monster this card is equipped to has its Type changed to anything but Warrior-Type, this card is destroyed. 11. If the opponent chains "Mystical Space Typhoon" to the activation of this card, this card is destroyed before its effect can be applied.
