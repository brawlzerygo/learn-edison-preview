Soul Exchange

Spell · Normal

Banlist: Unlimited (3 copies)

Edison text

① Target 1 monster your opponent controls; this turn, if you Tribute a monster, you can Tribute that monster, as if you controlled it. (You can't conduct your Battle Phase the turn you activate this card).

Printed text (newer wording, may work differently)
Target 1 monster your opponent controls; this turn, if you Tribute a monster, you must Tribute that target, as if you controlled it. You can't conduct your Battle Phase the turn you activate this card.

Edison rulings

● Even if that monster becomes flipped face-down, you can still tribute it. ● You are not forced to tribute that monster. You can tribute other monsters instead. ● Face-down monsters don't have Types nor Attributes. You can't tribute an opponent's face-down monster for a Ritual Summon, not for "Icarus Attack". ● If this card's activation is negated, you can still conduct your Battle Phase. ● If this card's effect is negated, you can't conduct your Battle Phase. ● You can use this card to tribute an opponent's face-up Plant for "Lonefire Blossom". ● You can't use this card to tribute an opponent's monster for your "Exiled Force".

General rulings
1. "Soul Exchange" targets. Select the target at activation. 2. "Soul Exchange" allows you to Tribute the targeted monster as though it was yours for the rest of the turn. 3. You can Tribute the targeted monster for any card that requires a Tribute, or for a Tribute Summon. So you could Tribute it for the effect of "Cannon Soldier", a Ritual Summon, or for the cost of "Monster Gate". 4. "Soul Exchange" does NOT give you control of your opponent's monster, so you can use "Soul Exchange" combined with the effect of your "Cannon Soldier" even if you already have 5 monsters in your Monster Card Zone. 5. You can activate 2 copies of "Soul Exchange" and target 2 different monsters. You could Tribute them both for the Tribute Summon of a Level 7 or higher monster. 6. You cannot activate "Soul Exchange" during your Main Phase 2 because you must skip your Battle Phase. 7. You can target a face-down monster, but you cannot Tribute it for a Ritual Summon or for "Metamorphosis" because you do not know its Level. 8. You cannot use "Soul Exchange" for "Polymerization" because "Polymerization" does not use Tribute. 9. If the activation of your "Soul Exchange" is negated (by "Solemn Judgment", etc.), you may conduct your Battle Phase. If only the effect of "Soul Exchange" is negated (by "Imperial Order", etc.), you may not conduct your Battle Phase. 10. You cannot Tribute the monster targeted by "Soul Exchange" to pay the cost to activate the effect of your "Exiled Force", because "Exiled Force" must Tribute itself.
