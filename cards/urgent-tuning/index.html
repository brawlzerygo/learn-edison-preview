Urgent Tuning

Trap · Normal

Banlist: Unlimited (3 copies)

Edison text

No Edison-Accurate PSCT available for this card.

Printed text (newer wording, may work differently)
During the Battle Phase: Immediately after this effect resolves, Synchro Summon 1 Synchro Monster using monsters you control.

Edison rulings

Activate only during the Battle Phase. Synchro Summon 1 Synchro Monster. (Send the appropriate Synchro Material Monsters to the GY.) ●This card does not target. ●This card can't be activated during the Damage Step. ●The Synchro Summon occurs when this effect resolves, it does not occur immediately after this effect resolves. as seen in the most recent erratum. ●Solemn Judgment can't negate the Synchro Summon performed when this effect resolves. ●If the number of monsters you control changes due to an effect chained to “Urgent Tuning”, and you don't have a Synchro Monster of a Level you could Summon when “Urgent Tuning” resolves, you must show your Extra Deck to your opponent to prove that you can't Summon a monster. You don't have to show your Extra Deck to your opponent if you would not be able to Synchro Summon at all when “Urgent Tuning” resolves. (Because you control only 1 monster, or control only Tuner monsters, etc.) ●You can use monsters that are not affected by Trap Cards, like “Elemental Hero Wildheart” as Synchro Material Monsters for “Urgent Tuning”.

General rulings
1. This card does not target. 2. This card cannot be activated during the Damage Step. 3. If the number of monsters you control changes due to an effect chained to "Urgent Tuning", and you do not have a Synchro Monster of a Level you could Summon when "Urgent Tuning" resolves, you must show your Extra Deck to your opponent to prove that you cannot Summon a monster. You do not have to show your Extra Deck to your opponent if you would not be able to Synchro Summon at all when "Urgent Tuning" resolves. (Because you control only 1 monster, or control only Tuner monsters, etc.) 4. You can use monsters that are not affected by Trap Cards, like "Elemental HERO Wildheart" as Synchro Material Monsters for "Urgent Tuning".
