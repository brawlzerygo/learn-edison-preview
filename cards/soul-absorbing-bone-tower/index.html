Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower

Zombie / Effect · DARK · Level 3 · 400 ATK / 1500 DEF

Banlist: Unlimited (3 copies)

Edison text

No Edison-Accurate PSCT available for this card.

Printed text (newer wording, may work differently)
If you control another Zombie monster, your opponent can't target this card for attacks. Each time a Zombie monster(s) is Special Summoned: Send the top 2 cards of your opponent's Deck to the GY.

Edison rulings

●If you have a Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower equipped with Raregold Armor and a Zombie-Type monster, your opponent can't attack. Even if you have a Zombie-Type monster, Gemini Elf, and a Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower equipped with Raregold Armor, your opponent can't attack. ●If you have another Zombie-Type monster and Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower, your opponent can't attack the Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower, but if you equip the Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower with Ring of Magnetism your opponent can declare an attack but must attack the Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower equipped with the Ring of Magnetism. ●If you have multiple Soul-Absorbing Bone Towers and no other monsters, your opponent can't attack. ●When your opponent attacks your Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower and during the Battle Step you activate Call of the Haunted to Special Summon another Zombie-Type monster, then a replay occurs (and you send 2 cards from your opponent's Deck to the GY). ●If DNA Surgery is in play changing all monsters to Zombie-Types, and a monster that was not originally a Zombie-Type is Special Summoned, Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower's effect is applied and you send 2 cards from your opponent's Deck to the GY. ●If a Zombie-Type monster is Special Summoned on your opponent's side of the field, Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower's effect activates. ●If a Zombie-Type monster is Special Summoned face-down (with The Shallow Grave, etc.) then Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower's effect does not activate. ●If multiple Zombie-Type monsters are Special Summoned simultaneously, Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower's effect is only applied once (so send just 2 cards). ●If Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower is Special Summoned by itself, its effect is not applied for its own Special Summon. ●If Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower and several other Zombie-Type monsters are Special Summoned simultaneously (with Cyber Jar), that Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower's effect is not applied. ●If you have Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower in play and The Kick Man is Special Summoned, Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower's effect is Step 1 of a chain and The Kick Man's effect is Step 2. ●If you have Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower and Card of Safe Return, and a Zombie-Type monster is Special Summoned, Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower is Step 1 of the chain and Card of Safe Return is Step 2. ●You can chain to Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower's effect that sends 2 cards to the GY.

General rulings
1. If you have a "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" equipped with "Raregold Armor" and a Zombie-Type monster, your opponent cannot attack. Even if you have a Zombie-Type monster, "Gemini Elf", and a "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" equipped with "Raregold Armor", your opponent cannot attack. 2. If you have another Zombie-Type monster and "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower", your opponent cannot attack the "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower", but if you equip the "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" with "Ring of Magnetism" your opponent can declare an attack but must attack the "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" equipped with the "Ring of Magnetism". 3. If you have multiple copies of "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" and no other monsters, your opponent cannot attack. 4. When your opponent attacks your "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" and during the Battle Step you activate "Call of the Haunted" to Special Summon another Zombie-Type monster, then a replay occurs (and you send 2 cards from your opponent's Deck to the GY). 5. If "DNA Surgery" is in play changing all monsters to Zombie-Types, and a monster that was not originally a Zombie-Type is Special Summoned, the effect of "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" is applied and you send 2 cards from your opponent's Deck to the GY. 6. If a Zombie-Type monster is Special Summoned on your opponent's side of the field, the effect of "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" activates. 7. If a Zombie-Type monster is Special Summoned face-down (with "The Shallow Grave", etc.) then the effect of "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" does not activate. 8. If multiple Zombie-Type monsters are Special Summoned simultaneously, the effect of "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" is only applied once (so send just 2 cards). 9. If "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" is Special Summoned by itself, its effect is not applied for its own Special Summon. 10. If "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" and several other Zombie-Type monsters are Special Summoned simultaneously (with "Cyber Jar"), the effect of that "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" is not applied. 11. If you have "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" in play and "The Kick Man" is Special Summoned, the effect of "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" is Step 1 of a chain and the effect of "The Kick Man" is Step 2. 12. If you have "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" and "Card of Safe Return", and a Zombie-Type monster is Special Summoned, "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" is Step 1 of the chain and "Card of Safe Return" is Step 2. 13. You can chain to the effect of "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" that sends 2 cards to the GY. 14. [FORUM] "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower" would not count itself when Special Summoned. "Soul-Absorbing Bone Tower", similar to "Genex Ally Crusher", must already be face-up on the field when the monster is Summoned for you to be able to activate its effect.
