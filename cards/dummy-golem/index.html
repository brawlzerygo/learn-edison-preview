Dummy Golem

Rock / Effect / Flip · EARTH · Level 2 · 800 ATK / 800 DEF

Banlist: Unlimited (3 copies)

Edison text

No Edison-Accurate PSCT available for this card.

Printed text (newer wording, may work differently)
FLIP: Your opponent selects 1 monster they control. Switch control of the selected monster and this card.

Edison rulings

●If “Dummy Golem” is attacked, flipped, and destroyed by damage calculation, its effect is not applied because it is destroyed. So control does not switch. ●If an effect like “Ring of Destruction” is chained to the Flip Effect of “Dummy Golem”, destroying “Dummy Golem”, the effect of “Dummy Golem” disappears. ●If the opponent only has one monster, and it is “Blindly Loyal Goblin” or “Mataza the Zapper”, and you activate the Flip Effect of “Dummy Golem”, the effect does activate and can be chained to, however its effect disappears because control of “Blindly Loyal Goblin” can't switch. ●If the opponent has “Blindly Loyal Goblin” and “Gemini Elf” on the field, and you activate the Flip Effect of “Dummy Golem”, the opponent can't select “Blindly Loyal Goblin”. They must select “Gemini Elf”. ●If control of monsters switches with “Dummy Golem”, it stays switched as long as the cards remain on the field. (Like “Creature Swap”.) ●The effect of “Dummy Golem” won't resolve if any of the following is true: “Dummy Golem” is not on the field when the effect resolves; “Dummy Golem” has been destroyed before the effect resolves, such as by damage calculation; there is no opponent’s monster when the effect resolves, such as if it was returned to the hand with “Compulsory Evacuation Device”.

General rulings
1. If "Dummy Golem" is attacked, flipped, and destroyed by damage calculation, its effect is not applied because it is destroyed. So control does not switch. 2. If an effect like "Ring of Destruction" is chained to the Flip Effect of "Dummy Golem", destroying "Dummy Golem", the effect of "Dummy Golem" disappears. 3. If the opponent only has one monster, and it is "Blindly Loyal Goblin" or "Mataza the Zapper", and you activate the Flip Effect of "Dummy Golem", the effect does activate and can be chained to, however its effect disappears because control of "Blindly Loyal Goblin" cannot switch. 4. If the opponent has "Blindly Loyal Goblin" and "Gemini Elf" on the field, and you activate the Flip Effect of "Dummy Golem", the opponent cannot select "Blindly Loyal Goblin". They must select "Gemini Elf". 5. If control of monsters switches with "Dummy Golem", it stays switched as long as the cards remain on the field. (Like "Creature Swap".) 6. The effect of "Dummy Golem" will NOT resolve if any of the following is true: (1) "Dummy Golem" is not on the field when the effect resolves; (2) "Dummy Golem" has been destroyed before the effect resolves, such as by damage calculation; (3) there is no opponent's monster when the effect resolves, such as if it was returned to the hand with "Compulsory Evacuation Device".
