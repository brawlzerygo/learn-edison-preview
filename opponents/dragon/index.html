Dragon Turbo

Combo · taught in Lesson 26

✕

Combo · Lists: Dragon Turbo, Dragon Aggro · Lesson 26

How to spot it

Draw Spells that discard Dragons: , and , then reviving a .

What it wants

Draw hard, fill the GY with big Dragons, then bring them back with Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragon, several times in one turn.

Strengths

  • It draws two cards at a time, turn after turn: Trade-In, Cards of Consonance and Super Rejuvenation.
  • 3000 ATK Blue-Eyes on the field fast.
  • turns a discard into a Blue-Eyes search.

Weaknesses

  • Red-Eyes and Blue-Eyes Special Summon: , and stop them.
  • stops White Stone's search.
  • banishes a Blue-Eyes before Red-Eyes revives it.

What to expect

  • Several Red-Eyes Darkness Metal Dragons in one turn.
  • sending five Dragons to the GY.
  • reviving White Stone to give Red-Eyes a Dragon to banish.

How to beat it

  • Cut off the Special Summons and the draws do nothing.
  • Save D.D. Crow for the Blue-Eyes they mean to revive.

Don't

  • Letting a turn of draws go unanswered: the next turn is the big one.
