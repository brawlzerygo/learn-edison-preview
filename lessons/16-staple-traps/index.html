Staple Traps

The Set cards that stop attacks, summons and big effects. 4 min · 6 flashcards

Traps that stop attacks

These go off when your opponent's monster declares an attack.

Traps that stop big effects

Starlight Road

(49%) answers cards that would destroy two or more of your cards: Heavy Storm, Torrential Tribute, Mirror Force, Black Rose Dragon. It negates that card and destroys it. Then you can Special Summon from your Extra Deck.

It must be chained right to the card that would destroy your cards, and it must be certain that two or more would be destroyed.

Royal Oppression

(38%, Semi-Limited) is a Continuous Trap. When a monster would be Special Summoned, or a card or effect that could Special Summon is activated, either player can pay 800 LP to negate it and destroy it.

Every Synchro Summon is a Special Summon, so Royal Oppression can stop every Synchro. It can even stop Starlight Road, because Road can summon Stardust. It cannot be used in the Damage Step, so it cannot stop Tragoedia.

The big idea

Royal Oppression is shared. Your opponent can pay to stop your summons too. Play it when you need Special Summons less than your opponent does.

Pulling the Rug

(44%) is a Counter Trap. It negates an effect that activates because a monster was Normal Summoned, and destroys that monster. That covers Caius (Tribute Summons are Normal Summons), Deep Sea Diva, Debris Dragon and Junk Synchron.

It cannot stop ignition effects, D.D. Crow, or effects from a Special Summon.

Common beginner mistakes
  • Activating Starlight Road against a card that destroys only one of your cards.
  • Forgetting that your opponent can pay for Royal Oppression too.
  • Using Dimensional Prison after the attack was already negated.
  • Thinking Mask of Restrict stops Synchro Summons. It does not.

Recap

  • Mirror Force and Dimensional Prison stop attacks.
  • Solemn Judgment, Bottomless and Torrential stop summons.
  • Starlight Road stops a card that would destroy 2+ of yours, then brings out Stardust.
  • Royal Oppression lets either player pay 800 to stop a Special Summon.
  • Pulling the Rug stops effects that trigger on a Normal Summon.
