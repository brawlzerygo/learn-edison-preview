Tuners and Synchro Summoning

Edison 101 · Lesson 2 of 9 · 10 min

The idea

Every Synchro Monster has a recipe printed on the card. Your field has Plaguespreader Zombie (Level 2, Tuner) and Cyber Dragon (Level 5). Add them up: 2 + 5 = 7, so you can make Black Rose Dragon.

The recipe: 1 Tuner + 1 or more non-Tuner monsters. The Levels must add up exactly.

The Level math

Add the Levels of all your ingredients. The total must equal the Synchro Monster's Level, not close to it.
